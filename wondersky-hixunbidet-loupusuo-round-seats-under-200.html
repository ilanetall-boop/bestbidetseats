Three Round-Bowl Electric Bidet Seats Under $200: WONDERSKY, HIXUNBIDET and Loupusuo

Most electric bidet seat listings on Amazon are built for elongated bowls, because most American toilets are elongated. That single fact quietly eliminates the bulk of the catalogue the moment you actually measure your own toilet and discover it's a round bowl — the shorter, more compact shape still common in older homes, half-baths and a lot of apartment construction. Search "electric bidet seat" and round-bowl options show up scattered among dozens of elongated ones, at every price from budget to luxury, and most don't bother to say which bowl shape they fit until you read the fine print.

Narrow that same search to electric seats that explicitly confirm a round fit, publish real specs instead of marketing copy, and stay under $200, and the list gets short fast. Three seats clear that bar with features worth comparing seriously: a WONDERSKY model with a panel-controlled LED display and a notably strict bolt-spacing requirement, a HIXUNBIDET model built entirely around remote control, and a Loupusuo seat that undercuts both on price and adds a child mode neither of the others includes. All three publish a confirmed round fit, an LED display, and more than two wash modes — which makes bowl shape and display type non-factors here. What's left to compare is bolt spacing, how you actually operate the seat day to day, and which one skips the one feature a household with kids might actually want.

One honest note before we get into it: we don't run a testing lab and haven't physically installed any of these three seats. Everything below comes from each product's own Amazon listing — its title and bullet points — compared side by side against our product database. Where a listing doesn't name a spec, we say so rather than guess at it.

Listing #1

WONDERSKY: an LED display, four wash modes, and a strict bolt-spacing window

WONDERSKY's listing leads with a warning most round-bowl shoppers will be glad to see before they click "buy," not after: "Measuring Before Ordering — please measure and confirm clearly that this toilet seat matches your toilet. The distance from the bolt holes to the front of the toilet bowl must be: 16.41"–17.55"." That's a window a little over an inch wide. Most round-bowl seats on Amazon either skip a bolt-spacing figure entirely or publish a looser range; WONDERSKY's listing is unusually specific, which cuts both ways — it tells you exactly what to measure, but it also means a toilet that falls even slightly outside that band isn't a candidate, full stop.

Once fit is confirmed, the feature set is straightforward: a panel controller paired with an LED display lets you adjust seat temperature, warm water and warm-air drying without guessing at unlabeled buttons, and four distinct wash modes — Rear Wash, Feminine Wash, Strong Rear Wash and Soft Wash — cover the range from a gentle clean to a firmer one. The nozzles clean themselves automatically before and after each use. Notably, WONDERSKY's controls live on a side panel rather than a handheld remote, which is the opposite choice from HIXUNBIDET below — worth flagging if a remote, not a panel, is the convenience feature you actually want.

Listing #2

HIXUNBIDET: four-level pressure, temperature and nozzle position — all from the remote

HIXUNBIDET takes the opposite approach to controls. Its listing title spells out "Remote Control, LED Display, Self-Cleaning Nozzle, Night Light & Multiple Cleaning Modes (Round)," and the first feature bullet makes the remote the whole point: "Intelligent Remote Control — one-click switching of 4 cleaning modes (posterior wash, feminine wash, oscillating wash, turbo wash) via remote, with 4-level adjustable water pressure, temperature, and nozzle position for intuitive operation." Every meaningful setting — which of the four modes is running, how hard the spray is, how warm the water and seat are, and where the nozzle sits — is adjusted from the handheld remote rather than a side panel.

Beyond the remote, HIXUNBIDET's seat heater offers four discrete temperature levels (off, low, medium, high), and its warm-air dryer runs five adjustment levels — one more level of dryer control than either of the other two seats publishes. If a wall-mountable remote is specifically what appeals to you here rather than a handheld one left to roll off the tank lid, our guide to bidet seats with a wall-mountable remote covers whether that upgrade is worth it. One gap worth flagging plainly: unlike WONDERSKY and Loupusuo, HIXUNBIDET's listing doesn't publish a bolt-spacing figure anywhere in its title or bullet points. That's not evidence it won't fit a round bowl — the listing does confirm "(Round)" in its own title — but the specific measurement a careful shopper wants before ordering simply isn't stated. Check the images and Q&A on the live listing for that number rather than assuming it matches WONDERSKY's.

Listing #3

Loupusuo: the cheapest of the three, and the only one with a child mode

Loupusuo's listing names its own fit window with two separate measurements rather than one: "Toilet front-to-tank wall(A): less than 19.2 inch; Mounting hole distance(B): 4.4–8.2 inch." That's a fundamentally different way of describing fit than WONDERSKY's single bolt-to-front figure — Loupusuo cares about both how far the bowl extends from the tank and how far apart the mounting bolts sit, so measure both dimensions on your own toilet before assuming either seat's range applies to the other.

On features, Loupusuo matches the other two closely: an LED display, a side control panel, a heated seat, a warm-air dryer and self-cleaning nozzles. Where it pulls ahead is wash-mode variety and price. Its listing names five modes — auto wash, hip wash, feminine wash, turbo wash and child mode — "meeting the personalized cleaning needs of the whole family." Neither WONDERSKY's four modes (Rear Wash, Feminine Wash, Strong Rear Wash, Soft Wash) nor HIXUNBIDET's four modes (posterior, feminine, oscillating, turbo) name anything resembling a child-specific setting. If a reduced-pressure mode for a kid using the seat unsupervised matters to your household, Loupusuo is the only one of these three that names it outright — and our guide to what kid mode and eco mode actually change is worth reading before you assume every "child mode" label works the same way. At $159.97, it's also two cents cheaper than the other two — a gap too small to matter financially, but a useful tiebreaker alongside the feature it alone offers.

Before you order

Measure your own round bowl's bolt spacing — don't trust the thumbnail

"Round bowl" is not one fixed size, and these three listings prove it by publishing three different sets of numbers for essentially the same bowl shape. WONDERSKY wants 16.41"–17.55" from the bolt holes to the front of the bowl. Loupusuo wants a front-to-tank-wall distance under 19.2 inches and a separate 4.4"–8.2" mounting-hole spread. HIXUNBIDET confirms "Round" in its title but doesn't publish either figure. Treating any of the three as interchangeable because they share a bowl-shape label is exactly the mistake a strict bolt-spacing requirement is designed to catch after the fact, when the seat is already sitting unopened on your bathroom floor.

The measurement itself takes under a minute: with the lid and seat removed, measure from the center of the two mounting bolts at the back of the bowl to the outer front edge of the bowl for a bolt-to-front figure like WONDERSKY's, and measure the distance between the two bolt centers themselves for a mounting-hole figure like Loupusuo's. Write both numbers down before you shop, not after you've already added a seat to your cart. If you're also unsure whether your bowl is round or elongated to begin with, our comparison of round and elongated seats from Aquonderful and KADSKAN walks through that distinction with a different pair of brands, and the same measuring logic applies here. For HIXUNBIDET specifically, since neither figure appears on its own listing, use the live Amazon Q&A section or contact the seller directly before ordering — don't assume it matches either of the other two seats' numbers just because all three are labeled round.

The real gap

The price spread is two cents — so price isn't what's deciding this

It's worth saying plainly: WONDERSKY and HIXUNBIDET both list at $159.99, and Loupusuo sits at $159.97. That's a two-cent spread across all three — not a meaningful dollar gap in either direction, and nowhere near enough to be the reason you'd pick one over another. If you came in expecting a clear "cheap vs premium" story the way some bidet seat comparisons play out, this isn't that story. All three sit in essentially the same price tier, which means the entire decision rests on the things that actually differ: how you want to operate the seat day to day, whether your bowl's bolt spacing matches a published figure at all, and whether a child mode is a feature you'll use.

That's a more useful comparison than a price gap would have been, honestly — it's the same lesson our look at three seats sharing an identical $169.99 price tag turned up in LAUNS, ZingPOP and XCQQ, compared: once price stops being a factor, the published spec sheet is the only thing left to judge a bidet seat by. Here, that spec sheet points to a panel-and-display seat with a tight, specific fit window (WONDERSKY), a remote-first seat with no published fit figure (HIXUNBIDET), and a slightly cheaper seat with the only child mode in the group and two fit measurements instead of one (Loupusuo). None of the three nozzles' materials are specified in any of these listings either — if that's a spec you care about, our breakdown of stainless steel vs. unspecified nozzle material on bidet seat listings explains what an unstated material does and doesn't tell you.

So the actual recommendation is simple: pick WONDERSKY if your toilet's bolt-to-front measurement lands inside 16.41"–17.55" and you're fine with panel controls; pick HIXUNBIDET if a remote matters more to you than a stated fit figure, and you're willing to confirm fit yourself before ordering; pick Loupusuo if two of your household's users are kids, or if having two independent fit measurements to check against your own toilet gives you more confidence than a single number does. At essentially identical prices, there's no wrong choice here on cost — only a wrong choice on fit, which is exactly why measuring first matters more in this particular comparison than in most.

FAQ

WONDERSKY, HIXUNBIDET and Loupusuo: common questions

Are WONDERSKY, HIXUNBIDET and Loupusuo actually different prices?

Barely. As of our most recent database check, WONDERSKY and HIXUNBIDET both list at $159.99, and Loupusuo sits at $159.97 — a two-cent spread across all three. Price isn't a meaningful differentiator here; bolt spacing, control method and wash modes are.

Does HIXUNBIDET's seat fit my round bowl the same way WONDERSKY's does?

Not necessarily, and its listing doesn't say either way. WONDERSKY publishes a bolt-to-front measurement of 16.41"–17.55"; Loupusuo publishes a front-to-tank-wall limit under 19.2 inches plus a 4.4"–8.2" mounting-hole spread. HIXUNBIDET confirms "Round" in its title but doesn't publish a fit figure, so measure your own toilet and check the live listing's Q&A or images before ordering rather than assuming it matches either of the other two.

Which of the three has a child mode?

Only Loupusuo. Its listing names five wash modes — auto wash, hip wash, feminine wash, turbo wash and child mode — while WONDERSKY's four modes (Rear Wash, Feminine Wash, Strong Rear Wash, Soft Wash) and HIXUNBIDET's four modes (posterior, feminine, oscillating, turbo wash) don't include anything described as a child-specific setting.

Do all three use a remote, or a side panel?

Two different approaches, not three. WONDERSKY and Loupusuo are both operated from a side control panel paired with an LED display. HIXUNBIDET is built entirely around a handheld remote — its listing describes every mode, pressure level, temperature level and nozzle position as adjustable "via remote," with no side-panel operation mentioned.
